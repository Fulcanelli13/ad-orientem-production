## Mandatory photo gates before any production import

1. **Owner choice** for the exact original and visual composition (including earlier documented selection, if present); being on Commons or in an editorial `APPROVED` registry alone is not enough. Rejections remain permanent. Check other recorded owner-choice cohorts, not just R28.
2. **Licence and authorship:** recheck the original page/version and whether the licence/attribution permits use in the app; preserve direct photographer/source licence links and visible credit in each card and folio.
3. **Binary integrity:** acquire the exact research derivative (or the exact source-approved original for new derivative) and independently verify SHA-256, dimensions, orientation, subject and absence of crop/retouch. Repository blob existence is not a fresh byte-level checksum.
4. **Claude rendering fidelity:** only after approval, use the original `AO_Explore_AppDesign.html` image-card layout, with full-frame/appropriate editorial crop and legible text; compare screenshot at 320/390/430/desktop before acceptance. Never substitute unrelated or AI-generated photographs.
5. **Clean production:** introduce only selected image bytes and their narrow licensing metadata into a new draft commit; never import the 388-item research registry or demo pictures wholesale. No changes to canonical Place IDs/coordinates or inferred Mass times.

Until these gates are met, keep `media_status: "NO_IMAGE_TRANSFERRED"` for **all 28** production pilot records.
